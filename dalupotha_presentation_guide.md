## Slide 2 — The Problem We Identified
**Heading:** What Was Happening Before
**Content:**
- Collection records were written by hand in paper registers every day
- Suppliers had no way to check their balances, requests, or payments
- Transport Agents had no digital tools — weights were noted on paper and reported later
- Managers only found out about issues the next day — or not at all
- Payment calculations for each supplier took hours to do manually

**Layout:** Clean bullet list with a paper-register or old logbook image on the side
**Screenshot:** ❌ None — use a stock photo of paper records / manual ledger

## Slide 3 — Our Solution: Two Unified Platforms
**Heading:** Two Connected Platforms. One Complete System.
**Important Facts:**
- **Real-Time Sync:** Data moves from field to office in seconds.
- **Role-Based Access:** 5 distinct dashboards for every estate department.
- **Unified Mobile App:** One app for TAs and Suppliers with dual login roles.

**Tagline at bottom:** *Eliminating the gap between field operations and management oversight.*

---

## Slide 4 — Mobile App: Transport Agent Role
**Heading:** The Field Agent View — Efficient Collections
**Important Facts:**
- **Bluetooth Scale Integration:** Weights captured automatically from the scale (no manual typing errors).
- **100% Offline Mode:** Record collections in remote fields with zero signal; data syncs once back in range.
- **GPS Verification:** Every collection is geo-tagged to prove the Agent was on-site.
- **Supplier Identification:** Fast login via Passbook QR codes or RFID cards.

**Simple one-liner:** *"Accurate, verified, and paperless field collections."*

---

## Slide 5 — Mobile App: Supplier Role
**Heading:** The Supplier View — Transparency in Your Pocket
**Important Facts:**
- **Dispute Reduction:** Suppliers see their exact supply history, debt, and balance instantly.
- **Request Tracking:** Real-time status updates on Fertilizer, Advance, and Tool requests.
- **Direct Communication:** Receive TRI circulars and official estate notices on the app.
- **GPS Tracking:** See the real-time location of the collection agent during their rounds.

**Simple one-liner:** *"Reducing office calls by putting data in the supplier's hands."*

---

## Slide 6 — Web Dashboard: Management Overview
**Heading:** Management Dashboard — Estate Command Center
**Important Facts:**
- **Live Operational KPIs:** Instant view of today's total leaf weight and route progress.
- **Bilingual Interface:** 100% support for both English and Sinhala.
- **Intelligent Alerts:** Automated notifications for low stock, pending approvals, and agent delays.
- **Centralized Data:** Replaces the traditional "Brought Leaf Register" with a searchable digital database.

**Simple one-liner:** *"Complete visibility of the entire estate from one central screen."*

---

## Slide 7 — Quality Assessment & Deductions
**Heading:** Precision Quality Control
**Important Facts:**
- **Automated Calculations:** System calculates net weight and quality deductions automatically.
- **Grading History:** Factory staff assign grades (A, B, C) which are instantly recorded.
- **Transparent Records:** Suppliers see their quality grades and deductions on their mobile app immediately.
- **Audit Ready:** Every quality assessment is timestamped and linked to the specific collection.

**Simple one-liner:** *"Fair, accurate, and documented quality grading for every kilogram."*

---

## Slide 8 — Financial Management & Payments
**Heading:** Automated Payments & Ledger Accuracy
**Important Facts:**
- **One-Click Payouts:** Generate monthly statements for all suppliers automatically.
- **Intelligent Deductions:** System automatically subtracts advances and store credit from final payments.
- **Debt Ageing Reports:** Track outstanding balances and debt recovery in real time.
- **Digital Audit Trail:** Every payment and deduction is logged permanently for audit compliance.

**Simple one-liner:** *"Simplified bookkeeping and guaranteed payment accuracy."*

---

## Slide 9 — Inventory & Stock Control
**Heading:** Smart Inventory Management
**Important Facts:**
- **Reorder Alerts:** Automatic warnings before fertilizer or bags run out.
- **Controlled Issuance:** Stocks only leave the warehouse when a digital approval is granted.
- **Audit-Ready History:** Full traceability of where every bag of fertilizer went.
- **Low-Stock Dashboards:** Instant visibility for the Store Keeper to prevent shortages.

**Simple one-liner:** *"Controlled stock, zero waste, and automated reorder tracking."*
